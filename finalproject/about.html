Projects & Entrepreneurship

Building beyond the lab

Founder & CEO

Restless Minds

Restless Minds is a cybersecurity and digital-protection company focused on helping small businesses and microenterprises access practical security services without unnecessary complexity. The project grew from the goal of making cybersecurity more understandable and accessible to organizations that may not have dedicated technical staff or large security budgets.

Its work is centered on practical security audits, digital protection, security education, and guidance that helps clients understand how to protect their systems, accounts, and physical access environments.

Developer

PortHunter

PortHunter is a lightweight Bash tool I developed to automate fast port discovery and service enumeration with Nmap. It combines full TCP discovery with optional deeper service scanning, automatic extraction of open ports, multi-target support, and workflow features designed to reduce repetitive reconnaissance steps.
